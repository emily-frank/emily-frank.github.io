I am a mixed-methods researcher studying how refugee reception policies shape refugees' mental health and well-being in their destination country, with a focus on Germany. I aim to conduct policy-relevant research that informs evidence-based policymaking and improves the daily lives of immigrants and refugees.

I recently joined Hochschule Hannover and the German Institute for Economic Research (DIW Berlin) as a postdoctoral researcher on the [SUARE project](https://www.diw.de/en/diw_01.c.866701.en/projects/longitudinal_study_of_ukrainian_refugees_in_germany__suare.html). In this role, I study experiences of discrimination at school and mental health within families among immigrant and refugee youth in Germany. I am also completing a postdoc in the Research Group Health and Social Inequality at the [WZB Berlin Social Science Center](https://www.wzb.eu/en). There, I use quantitative methods to study the determinants of immigrants' mental health as part of an interdisciplinary, mixed-methods [project](https://einstein-diversity.com/research/migrant-families-mental-health/) of the [Einstein Center Population Diversity](https://einstein-diversity.com/) that focuses on immigrants' mental health in a family context. 

I received my PhD in 2024 from [DYNAMICS](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/en/dynamics), a joint Research Training Group of [Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin](https://www.hu-berlin.de/en) and the [Hertie School](https://www.hertie-school.org/en/). My mixed-methods dissertation examined how legal status shapes refugees' experiences of citizenship and belonging at German welfare offices. During my PhD, I also worked as a research associate at the Berlin Institute for Empirical Integration and Migration Research ([BIM](https://www.bim.hu-berlin.de/en/bim-start)), where I studied public attitudes toward immigration and welfare chauvinism in the project [Migration and the Welfare State](https://www.projekte.hu-berlin.de/de/migsoz).

I hold a bachelor's degree in sociology from New York University and a master's degree in public policy from the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

On this website, you can find more about my research projects and download my [CV](/cv).
