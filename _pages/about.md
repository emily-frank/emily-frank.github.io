As a researcher, I am interested in how refugee reception policies shape their experiences of mental health and well-being in their destination country, with a focus on Germany. I aim to conduct policy-relevant research that can shape evidence-informed policymaking and improve immigrants' daily lives. 

I am currently an incoming postdoctoral researcher at the Hochschule Hannover and German Institute for Economic Research (DIW), where I've recently joined the [SUARE project](https://www.diw.de/en/diw_01.c.866701.en/projects/longitudinal_study_of_ukrainian_refugees_in_germany__suare.html). In this role, I study experiences of discrimination at school and mental health in the family context among immigrant and refugee youth in Germany. I am also wrapping up a postdoc at the [WZB Berlin Social Science Center](https://www.wzb.eu/en), Research Group Health and Social Inequality, where I use quantitative approaches to study determinants of immigrants' mental health as part of a project of the [Einstein Center Population Diversity](https://einstein-diversity.com/). [Our project](https://einstein-diversity.com/research/migrant-families-mental-health/) is interdisciplinary and mixed methods, focusing particularly on the role of immigrants' families in shaping mental health outcomes. I consider myself a mixed-methods researcher.

In 2024, I defended my PhD, which I completed in the [DYNAMICS](https://www.sowi.hu-berlin.de/en/dynamics)) program, a joint Research Training Group of the [Humboldt University Berlin](https://www.hu-berlin.de/en) and the [Hertie School](https://www.hertie-school.org/en/). During that time, I also worked a research associate at the Berlin Institute for Empirical Integration and Migration Research ([BIM](https://www.bim.hu-berlin.de/en/bim-start)). In my mixed methods dissertation, I examined how legal status shapes refugees' experiences of citizenship and belonging at German welfare offices. For my work at BIM, I studied public attitudes on immigration and welfare chauvinism as part of a project called [Migration and the Welfare State](https://www.projekte.hu-berlin.de/de/migsoz).

I completed my bachelor's degree in sociology at New York University and my master's degree in public policy at the University of Wisconsin-Madison.

On this website, you can find more information on my research projects and download my [CV](/cv).
